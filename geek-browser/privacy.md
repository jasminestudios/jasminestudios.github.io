# Privacy Policy

**Geek Browser** · Last updated: 2026-09-12

This privacy policy explains what information Geek Browser handles, where it
goes, and what choices you have. Geek Browser is published by **Jasmine Studios**.

If anything below is unclear, please contact us at [support@jasminestudios.net](mailto:support@jasminestudios.net).

---

## TL;DR

* Geek Browser has **no account** and **no server of ours**. We never see your browsing.
* The **app** contains **no** analytics or tracking SDK. (Our marketing website uses basic Google Analytics — see "Website analytics" below.)
* History, bookmarks, tabs, settings, saved commands and the assistant's memory files live **on your iPad** and, if you turn on iCloud sync, in **your own iCloud account**.
* The AI assistant sends the **text of the page you are on, your question, and the tabs you @-mention** to the AI provider **you** chose. With Apple Intelligence on-device, nothing leaves the iPad.
* **Passwords are never sent to any AI provider.** Sign-in happens in the page with iCloud Keychain; the assistant stops at sign-in pages and hands you the tab.
* We do not sell, share, or resell anything.

## What information Geek Browser handles

### 1. Browsing data you create

History, bookmarks, open tabs, per-site settings, saved commands and key
bindings are stored on your device. If you enable iCloud, some of them sync
through your private iCloud container, which only your Apple ID can read.
Jasmine Studios has no access to it.

Websites you visit see what any browser tells them (your IP address, the
user agent, cookies they set). Geek Browser ships a content blocker, on by
default, that removes common ad and tracker requests; you can turn it off
per site or globally in Settings › Privacy.

### 2. AI assistant (optional, user-initiated)

Nothing is sent until you ask the assistant something or give it a task.
When you do, the request contains:

* the text and structure of the page in the current tab (and the ✦ working tab while a task runs),
* your question or instruction,
* the text of any other open tab you @-mention,
* your standing preferences and the notes for the current site from the memory files (the daily log is **not** included).

It goes to the provider you selected in Settings › AI Assistant:

* **Apple Intelligence (on-device)** — processed on your iPad; nothing is transmitted. Off by default; turn on "Reading tasks on this device".
* **Claude, OpenAI, Gemini or DeepSeek** — with **your own API key**, sent directly from the app to that provider under their privacy terms. Your key is kept in the device Keychain and is not synced to iCloud.

Before a consequential action — leaving the site, submitting a form,
purchasing, posting, opening tabs, saving a command — the assistant asks
first, unless you chose Full access for that run. The assistant never
receives passwords or passkeys.

Memory files are plain markdown in the app's Documents folder on the
device. You can open, edit, or delete them at any time.

### 3. Website analytics

Our marketing website (**jasminestudios.net/geek-browser**) uses **Google
Analytics** to measure basic, aggregate visit statistics (page views,
approximate region, referrer). This applies to the website only — **not**
to the app — and is subject to
[Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). You can
block it with any content blocker or by opting out of analytics cookies.

## Your choices

* **Clear browsing data** — Settings › Privacy lets you clear history, cookies and website data.
* **Stop using the assistant** — Simply don't ask it anything; the browser works fully without it. Or keep only "Reading tasks on this device" on, so nothing leaves the iPad.
* **Remove an API key** — Settings › AI Assistant › your provider › Remove key.
* **Delete memory files** — Settings › AI Assistant › Memory, or delete the files in the app's Documents folder via the Files app.
* **Stop using iCloud sync** — Turn off iCloud for Geek Browser in iPadOS Settings → Apple ID → iCloud.
* **Remove the app** — Long-press the icon → Delete App. This removes the on-device data. iCloud-stored data remains in your account until you remove it via Settings → Apple ID → iCloud → Manage Storage.
